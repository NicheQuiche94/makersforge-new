02 · Candidates

Airtable, in three steps

  1. Create the record

    Every candidate goes into Airtable. Fill out the form fields as you go.

  2. Raw notes during the call

    Take meeting notes straight into the raw notes section. Messy is fine, this is the transcript-ish version.

  3. Convert to a spec afterwards

    Turn the raw notes into a clean spec in the recruiter notes section. This is the version anyone else can pick up and use.

Why both sections exist

Raw notes preserve what was actually said, including the throwaway lines that matter later. The spec is what makes the record useful to someone who was not on the call. Keeping them separate means neither job gets done badly.

03 · Leads

Folk, and how things get into it

  • Automatically. Roles ingested by the job board from around 500 tracked companies land in Folk enriched, every day.
  • From LinkedIn. Install the Folk browser extension. Anything you find on LinkedIn can be pulled straight in.
  • By hand. Anything you come across elsewhere. If it is a relevant role and not already in Folk, add it, and it flows through to the board.

Day 10 and day 20

Roles still open at those marks get flagged. They are the easiest place to start a working session. Full detail in the Job Funnel manual.

Enrichment

  • Folk enriches data, but on limited credits.
  • When you add a lead, fill in anything you already know, particularly an email address.
  • Don't run enrichment yourself. Andre runs it in batches every few days so the credits last.

04 · Email

From your own address, one at a time

  • Send from your MakersForge Outlook address, not from Folk.
  • Individually, never through a merge tool or sequencer.
  • Log replies worth noting in the outreach channel in Slack.

This lines up with the Cold Outreach manual, and it is a positioning choice rather than a technical limitation. Small batches, written properly, sent by a person.

05 · Affiliates

What you run, and what comes to Andre

Individual affiliates

  • Yours to recruit, brief, and manage.
  • Request the agreement from Andre, and it goes out via DocuSign.
  • Record them properly: name, email, company, type, segment, who recruited them, date. Your name in that record is what triggers your override.
  • Their leads come to you, and you get them into Folk.

Agency partners

  • Same recruitment and agreement process.
  • The difference is the giveaway: a 45-minute brief and interview process health check, run by Andre personally.
  • When an agency wants to use it for a client, loop Andre in and hand over the context.
Still being finalised

Exactly how the health check gets booked, whether via a direct introduction, a booking link, or Andre reaching out, is not settled yet. For now, flag it to Andre and he will take it from there.

06 · Habits

The small things that keep it working

Log promptly, not perfectlyBatching a week into one session is fine. What matters is that it gets in, because CRM records are the starting point for working out origination credit.
Add what the system missedA role you spot elsewhere takes a minute to add and becomes a prospect for all of us.
Share repliesAnything that landed, and anything you were confident about that didn't. Slack, one line.
Say when something's brokenIf a tool is missing, a view is wrong, or a process is costing you time, that is worth more to me than working around it quietly.
